Wild Animal Detection System

A live model of the prototype: an ESP32 DevKit reads two ultrasonic sensors at 4 ft and 6 ft, sounds the buzzer when only the lower beam is blocked, and reports to the Wild Sync console.

Circuit on the control board

The wiring as built: ESP32 DevKit, two HC-SR04 modules with a shared echo line, a 1k and 2k divider, and the buzzer.

Echo outputs are tied together, so the firmware triggers one sensor at a time. If readings look odd on the real board, give each sensor its own echo pin.
